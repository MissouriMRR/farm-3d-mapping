# Glossary

**Field**
A farm land parcel that gets surveyed, e.g. Callis Road (Field 175).
_Avoid_: farm (a farm holds many fields), site

**Flight**
One drone pass over a Field and the photo set it produces.
_Avoid_: mission, dataset

**Survey**
A Flight's photos together with everything processed from them. A Survey is what the viewer displays.
_Avoid_: project, run, task

**Height layer**
A measured elevation product of a Survey: the mesh, the DSM (surface model), the DTM (bare-ground model) or canopy height (DSM minus DTM). Values read from it are measurements with a stated datum.
_Avoid_: 3D model (ambiguous with the Splat), terrain (that means the global base terrain)

**Splat**
The Gaussian-splat layer of a Survey. It shows what the Field looks like and is never used for measurement.
_Avoid_: 3D scan, point cloud
